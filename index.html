Forgeries Slip Onto the Word Page

Every entry on today’s page comes as two proofs, one real and one forged. Pick the real word to set it in type. A wrong pick goes to press as an erratum, and the third erratum pulls the page.

How to play

Tap the proof you think is real. Both are then revealed: where the real word comes from, and how the forgery was made. The entry then takes its place on the word page, the real word in type and the forgery struck out beneath it.

The words grow rarer down the page, from the familiar to the all but lost. A wrong pick goes to press as an erratum; at the third, the editor pulls the page.

A mountweazel is a fake entry planted in a reference work to catch copiers. With a keyboard, press ← or → to pick.
