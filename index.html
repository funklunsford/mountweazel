To the editor

Forgeries on Today’s Word Page

You’re the editor. Every entry on today’s word page has come back with a forged twin. Send the real word to press. Pass a forgery and you print an erratum; three errata, and the page is pulled.

How to play

Each entry reaches your desk as two proofs. Tap the one you think is real. Both are then revealed: where the real word comes from, and how the forgery was made. The entry takes its place on your page, the real word in type and the forgery struck out beneath it.

The words grow rarer down the page, from the familiar to the all but lost. Pass a forgery and it goes to press as an erratum; at the third erratum, the page is pulled.

A mountweazel is a fake entry planted in a reference work to catch copiers. With a keyboard, press ← or → to pick.
