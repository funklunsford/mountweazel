Expedition

Twelve Ledges Up Mount Weazel

Two old words wait on every ledge, one real and one forged. Pick the real one to climb, and it’s set on the word page. You carry three ropes; a wrong pick cuts one.

How to play

Tap the word you think is real. Both are then revealed: where the real word comes from, and how the forgery was made. Climb on, and the ledge is set on the word page: the real entry in type, the forgery struck out.

The words grow rarer and stranger as you climb, from the foothills to the summit at 12,000 feet. A wrong pick cuts a rope, and the forgery goes to press with an erratum, but you keep climbing. Lose all three ropes and the climb ends where you stand.

A mountweazel is a fake entry planted in a reference work to catch copiers. With a keyboard, press ← or → to pick.
