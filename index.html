Mountweazel

Climb No. 1

Two old words on every ledge, one real and one forged. Grab the real one to climb. You carry three ropes, and a wrong grab cuts one.

How to climb
  1. 1

    Tap the word you think is real. Both cards then turn over: where the real word comes from, and how the forgery was made.

  2. 2

    A wrong grab cuts a rope, but you keep climbing. Lose all three ropes and the climb ends where you stand.

  3. 3

    The words grow rarer and stranger as you climb, from the foothills to the summit at 12,000 feet. With a keyboard, press ← or → to grab.
