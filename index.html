Expedition

Twelve Ledges Up Mount Weazel

On every ledge wait two old words, one real and one forged. Pick the real one to climb on. You carry three ropes, and each wrong pick cuts one.

How to play

Tap the word you think is real. Then both are revealed: where the real word comes from, and how the forgery was made. Climb on when you’re ready.

The words grow rarer and stranger as you climb, from the foothills to the summit at 12,000 feet. A wrong pick cuts a rope, but you keep climbing; lose all three and the climb ends where you stand.

A mountweazel is a fake entry planted in a reference work to catch copiers. With a keyboard, press ← or → to pick.
